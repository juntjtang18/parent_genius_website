Why Families Trust Us.

Every parent's journey begins with a question: "Am I ready? Am I doing this right?" Parenting doesn't come with a manual. And that feeling is universal: the need for support, reassurance, and guidance that actually fits your life. You don't have to figure it out alone.

Our Care Belief

GeniusParentingAI exists to help parents build strong foundational knowledge, avoid common pitfalls, and make thoughtful, informed decisions that support their children’s long-term well-being.

By combining educational expertise, technological innovation, and real-life parenting insight, our platform aims to benefit families, educators, practitioners, and institutions — while also supporting research, communication, and collaboration across the broader child and family well-being community.

Our Vision

To empower parents with the knowledge, tools, and support they need to create loving, supportive families that nurture growth, foster resilience, and inspire the next generation to thrive with confidence, compassion, and curiosity.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide parents with actionable insights, expert advice, and innovative resources, helping them create nurturing environments where children can grow, thrive, and develop the skills they need to succeed in life.

Shu Guo

Founder, GeniusParentingAI
Master of Education, University of British Columbia

Shu Guo is an educator with a Master of Education from the University of British Columbia and extensive experience working with children and families.

GeniusParentingAI was not born from the idea of perfect parenting — but from real, lived experience. Like many parents, Shu encountered confusion, mistakes, self-doubt, and moments of uncertainty in her own parenting journey.

Through these challenges, she developed a core belief: failure is not the opposite of good parenting — it is often where meaningful learning begins.

This philosophy shapes the foundation of GeniusParentingAI: compassionate support, practical guidance, and evidence-informed insight for real families facing real challenges.
